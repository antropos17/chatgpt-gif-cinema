THE DIRECTOR'S DESK / 03

Make a moment.
Then make
it matter.

A skill and local CLI for Codex and Claude Code. You direct the story. The toolkit keeps the takes, cuts, and exports in order.

  1. 01

    Find the action.

    One shot, one visible change. Choose the character, the look, and what happens next.

  2. 02

    Choose your take.

    Generate deliberately, or bring existing GIFs. Review identity, movement, and the frames around each cut.

  3. 03

    Let the story play.

    Assemble an MP4. Add an editable title. Export small GIFs or whole-shot chapters.
