## Anime pixel art

The complete **100-character catalog and three outfit studies**, moved from the
homepage without changing the GIFs, stills or source drawings. Each card links to
its source method and limitations. These are short pose studies, not finished
15-second films or proof of seamless continuity.

This page references about **79 MB of animated media**. Open a **Still** link for
an individual pose, or return to the [lightweight project overview](../README.md).
