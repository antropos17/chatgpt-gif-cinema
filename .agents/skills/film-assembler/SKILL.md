# Film Assembler
Use `gif-cinema film output/01.gif output/02.gif -o output/movie.mp4`. Explicitly specify aspect ratio/fps, inspect audio sync, avoid frozen starts, repeated duplicate frames and mismatched pacing. Keep audio rights clear.
